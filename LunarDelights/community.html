Here, at Lunar Delights bakery, we strive to give back to the community. There are many unfortunate situations out there that may cause difficulties for some individuals so we try to focus on helping those that may need an extra hand.

We offer fundraising opportunities and schedule events to help give back to the community, including rounding up volunteers to assist at shelters, animal shelters and food banks.

If you, or anyone you may know, are interested in assisting with these opportunities please contact us. We have listed some events that we will host.

Thank you for your support!
